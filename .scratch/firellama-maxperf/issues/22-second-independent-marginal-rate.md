# 22: Second independent marginal rate

**What to build:** A second byte step at the same depth gives a marginal rate that does not descend from a single pair. The existing byte rate comes from one same-model quant pair, and every mechanism story built on it has been entangled with that single choice of probe. A second pair at a different width makes the rate falsifiable.

**Blocked by:** 20.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The second pair holds depth fixed and is reported with its own byte delta and its own noise floor
- [ ] The two same-model rates are reported side by side as separate observations, and are not averaged
- [ ] The result says whether the byte rate is stable across widths, and if not, what that does to the decomposition
- [ ] The quant family of each step is recorded, since dequantisation cost per byte differs between families
